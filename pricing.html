Common Questions About GatewayOfChess

How is pricing determined for our academy?

Pricing is tailored to the size of your academy, including active student enrollment, number of coaching staff, and whether you operate single or multiple branches.

Can we migrate our existing students and batch data?

Yes. GatewayOfChess provides assisted onboarding and batch data import tools to make transition smooth without disrupting ongoing classes.

Can we book a live demonstration first?

Absolutely. Our team provides personalized walkthroughs showing exactly how your academy's scheduling, coaching and student journeys will work.
